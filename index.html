Questions

Before you visit

Quick answers to what patients ask us most.

How do I book an appointment?

Call 7070003331 or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm a time that suits you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous X-rays, prescriptions or treatment records, and a list of medicines you take. This helps the doctor plan safely.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. After the examination, the doctor explains the options and costs so you can decide comfortably.
